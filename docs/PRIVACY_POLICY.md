# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 23 July 2026

Statement to Excel Kenya processes files entirely on your device. We do not
collect, transmit, store, sell, or share personal information, financial
information, PDF statements, statement passwords, or converted transaction data.

The app does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, cloud storage, or a remote
server. A selected statement is copied into temporary application storage so it can
be processed. After a successful conversion, the app attempts to delete that
temporary PDF copy. A converted Excel file is shared or saved only when you choose
a destination using your device's native share menu.

A failed or interrupted conversion may retain temporary files in the private
application cache until the operating system clears them or the app is removed.
The app also removes stale workbooks it generated when creating a new export.
Other apps can access an exported workbook only when you explicitly share or save
it to a location they can access.

This app is an independent community utility and is not affiliated with or endorsed
by Safaricom PLC.

Questions about this policy can be directed to the support contact listed on the
app's Google Play store page.
